About Us

Al Habbai is a progressively private owned company was established in Dubai since 1993 by Al Habbai Family and represented by CEO Mr. Ali Al Habbai. It is renowned for its integrity, teamwork and commitment. Moreover, as one of the leading contractors in Dubai, with permits for unlimited storey construction and approved to ISO 9001:2008 and 14001:2015, it has been active on many important and prestigious projects. Many of Habbai’s projects are found in Down Town, Business Bay, Media City, Palm Jumeirah, and other high profile destinations in Dubai.

Al Habbai Contracting strives to provide its clients with superior quality of customer satisfaction, intuitive scheduling, competitive pricing and superior product delivery. Its success is measured by team performance, strategic growth and excellence, all of which enable the company to continue building an unparalleled reputation for perfection.

GM

Al Habbai Contracting LLC is supported by a strong and highly experienced management team, making it one of the innovative and respected companies in the construction market.

Eng. Walid Abbas, the company’s General Manager since 2004, believes that its success is built on commitment, effective organization, teamwork, and continuous development. The company places great importance on transferring knowledge and experience from senior professionals to younger team members, creating a strong culture of teamwork, responsibility, and commitment.

Al Habbai Contracting LLC is dedicated to providing clients with excellent service, quality workmanship, and efficient project execution. The company continuously invests in modern equipment, employee development, education, and professional training. With extensive experience in the construction industry, Al Habbai has the capability to manage projects of various sizes from start to completion. By understanding each client’s individual requirements, the company provides practical, cost-effective solutions while maintaining high standards of quality, safety, and professionalism.
