Awards and News

Al Habbai Contracting LLC has recently signed a contract with M/s. Anwar Investment Limited, Dubai for the construction of a proposed (UG + GF + 2P + 8 Floors) residential building located on Plot No. 101-0581, Dubai Islands, Dubai, UAE.

Location

Dubai Islands, Dubai, UAE

Development By

Nakheel

Investor

M/s. Anwar Investment Limited, Dubai

The project is situated within Dubai Islands, a major coastal development by Nakheel, the renowned developer behind Palm Jumeirah and The World Islands. Dubai Islands form part of the city's strategic initiative to expand its coastline and enhance its tourism and real estate offerings.

The Islands will include cultural sites, entertainment hubs, and family-friendly facilities.
